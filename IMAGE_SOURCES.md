# Image Sources — N&N International @ Grain Market Stores

All images are stored locally in this project. No image is hotlinked.

## Supplied by N&N International (WhatsApp zip, 22 Oct 2026 — 27 market photographs)

The supplied photographs are grain-market scenes (open sacks and stalls), not
isolated product close-ups. They are used where the scene genuinely matches,
and in the "Inside the Grain Market" gallery without assigning a specific
variety to a scene that cannot be verified from the photograph alone.
